3. Signal Ratio (A, B, Γ)

The Experiment: How predictable is the world?

The researcher wanted to test how good their AI is under different conditions.
    -> To do this, they broke down the total "variance" (the total amount of randomness) into 3 buckets:
        + A (The Constant Part): How much of the demand is just the standard baseline? (θˉi)
        + B (The Weather Part): How much of the demand is actually caused by the weather? (The a.w_m.w_n part)
        + Γ (Gamma - The Noise Part): How much of the demand is just pure, unpredictable chaos? (ϵi)

    Why does it matter?
        + If B is high, the world is very predictable if you look at the weather. -> The AI should perform amazingly well because it can use the weather data to plan perfect routes.
        + If Γ is high, the world is just a chaotic casino.
            -> Even a perfect AI can't predict what's going to happen.
                -> The AI will struggle, but it will still try to minimize the damage -> by using "recourse" (driving back to the depot) efficiently.
